The Concept

Where Culinary Craft Meets
Untamed Adventure

The Wild Kitchen Show follows Chef Desh into the wild — cooking, exploring, and bringing food stories to life on screen. From remote coastlines to mountain camps, every episode is a journey into the relationship between nature and nourishment.

Currently in development for broadcast, Wild Kitchen is a Flavours Coast to Coast production — a TV series pitch that blends adventure travel with culinary storytelling in a way Canadian television hasn't seen before.

Desh Fernando brings his background as a chef, photographer, and outdoor adventurer together in a show that's as visually stunning as it is delicious.

Flavours Coast to Coast

The Vision

Wild Kitchen is more than a show — it's a platform for celebrating Canadian food culture, wild ingredients, and the chefs and communities who live closest to the land.

🏔️

Adventure Cooking

Cooking in the wild with foraged and locally-sourced ingredients, bringing restaurant-quality technique to the most remote settings.

🎬

Cinematic Storytelling

Every episode is shot with a photographer's eye for light and composition — stunning visuals that do justice to the landscapes and dishes.

🌿

Coast to Coast Canada

From Vancouver Island's old-growth forests to Atlantic shores — a journey through the flavours and food cultures that define this country.
