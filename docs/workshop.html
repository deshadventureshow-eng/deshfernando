Toronto Sourdough Workshop

Where Community
Meets Craft

It all began when Desh baked his first sourdough loaf in his condo — hooked by the taste, smell, and texture. A neighbour's simple suggestion turned into a workshop, and a community was born.

Since 2020, the Toronto Sourdough Workshop has brought together curious bakers of all levels, teaching the science and soul of fermented bread in an approachable, joyful environment. Whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned home baker, there's a seat at Desh's table.

The Curriculum

What You'll Learn

01

The Living Starter

Understand how to create, feed, and maintain a sourdough starter — your living ingredient that gives bread its character.

02

Fermentation Science

Learn why time and temperature matter, and how to harness natural fermentation to develop complex flavour and texture.

03

Shaping & Scoring

Master the hands-on techniques of shaping a proper boule and scoring with confidence — where art meets the oven.

04

Baking in a Home Oven

No professional equipment needed. Learn to get bakery-quality results from whatever oven you have at home.

05

Troubleshooting

Flat loaves, gummy crumb, over-proofed dough — we'll walk through common problems and how to fix them.

06

Beyond the Basic Loaf

Focaccia, pizza dough, sourdough pancakes — once you have the fundamentals, the possibilities are endless.
